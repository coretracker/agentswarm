The Sun is the [star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star) located at the centre of the [Solar System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_System). It is a massive sphere of hot [plasma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_(physics)), heated to [incandescence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incandescence) by [nuclear fusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_fusion) reactions in its core, radiating the energy from its [surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface) mainly as [visible light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visible_light) and [infrared radiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrared_radiation) with 10% at [ultraviolet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultraviolet)[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun#cite_note-18)[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun#cite_note-19)[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun#cite_note-20)[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun#cite_note-21) energies. It is the main source of energy for [life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life) on [Earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth). The Sun [has been an object of veneration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sun_in_culture) in many cultures and a central subject of astronomical research since [antiquity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_history).
